Wall	Gets in the way; can only be destroyed with a Bomb.
GhostStar	Combines with another Star, but disappears after you drop 5 more shapes.
Multiply	Combines with itself to create a Star.
Inverter	Changes the gravity of all shapes.
TrainTrack	Moves a shape to the very left or right of the level and resets it.
Bag	Creates 4 Cursed Circles on landing. (Cursed shapes can't combine with each other.)
Egg	Becomes a Star after you drop 5 more shapes, but disappears if you combine any shapes.
Snowflake	Disappears when you combine 4 shapes; shapes around this cannot be combined or destroyed.
Mirror	Curses a shape, then this becomes a Cursed copy of that shape. (Cursed shapes can't combine with each other.)
Chest	Transforms 4 Squares around this into Arrows.
BlackHole	Transforms 6 Circles around this into Cursed Squares. (Cursed shapes can't combine with each other.)
Cage	When 3 shapes combine at once, this transforms into a Circle, Square, and Arrow.
Tube	Moves a shape to the very top or bottom of the level and resets it.
Flashlight	When there are 10 shapes in the range, this transforms into a Star.
