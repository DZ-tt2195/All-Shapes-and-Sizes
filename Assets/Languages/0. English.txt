Bag	Creates $Num1$ Cursed Circles, and $Num2$ Cursed Squares. (Cursed shapes can't combine with each other.)

Cage	When this is destroyed by a Bomb, create a Circle, Square, and Arrow.
Chest	Transforms $Num$ Squares around this into Arrows, and destroys all Circles around this.
Egg	Transforms into a Star after you drop $Num$ more shapes, but disappears if you combine any shapes.
Flashlight	When there are $Num$ shapes in the range, this transforms into a Star.
GhostStar	Combines with another Star, but disappears after you drop $Num$ more shapes.
Inverter	Changes the gravity of all shapes.
Mirror	Curses a shape, then this becomes a Cursed copy of that shape. (Cursed shapes can't combine with each other.)
Multiply	Combines with itself to create a Star.
Swap	Transform all Arrows into Diamonds, and vice versa.
Chains	When $Num1$ shapes combine at once, create $Num2$ Cursed Arrows. (Cursed shapes can't combine with each other.)
Snowflake	Disappears when you combine $Num$ shapes; shapes around this cannot be combined or destroyed.
TrainTrack	Moves a shape to the very left or right of the level and resets it.
Tube	Moves a shape to the very top or bottom of the level and resets it.
Wall	Gets in the way; can only be destroyed with a Bomb.
